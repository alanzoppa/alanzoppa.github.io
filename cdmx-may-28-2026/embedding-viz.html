What is an Embedding?

A list of numbers (a vector) that represents a word or sentence in "meaning space." Words with similar meanings get similar number lists — even if they share no letters at all.

Why 4096 Dimensions?

Mnestic uses qwen3-embedding-8b producing 4096-dimensional vectors. High dimensions capture nuanced semantic relationships — distinguishing "promise" from "obligation" from "commitment."
