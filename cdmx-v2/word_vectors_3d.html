t-Distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding optimizes for local neighborhood preservation. Points close in 4096-dim space will be close here — but distances between clusters are meaningless. Two clusters appearing near each other does NOT mean they're semantically related. The algorithm warps global geometry to make local structure legible. KL divergence (0.9859) measures how well 3D neighbor probabilities match the originals. Kruskal stress (0.35) quantifies overall distance distortion. Best used for: spotting unexpected local clusters, not comparing group distances.

Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection attempts to preserve both local neighborhoods and some global structure — a middle ground between PCA and t-SNE. n_neighbors=7 controls how many local points define the manifold (low = very local); min_dist=0.1 controls how tightly points can pack. With 24 data points, UMAP has enough neighbors to build a more reliable manifold than with 16. The clusters you see are real, but inter-cluster distances are suggestive but not trustworthy — check the stress score (0.35) against PCA's (0.24).
