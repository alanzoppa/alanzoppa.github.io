field manual
The frostworm hunts by vibration. Your threat gauge (top-left) fills with noise:

• Boosting fills it fast. Normal driving fills it slowly.
• Idling or gliding slow lets it drain. Stationary = nearly silent.
• Blue rift valleys absorb sound — noise radiates 60% less inside them.

When the gauge maxes out, the worm surfaces and hunts your position. It re-locks onto you only while you keep making noise — go quiet and it tracks your last known spot. It cannot dig through the stone ridges between basins.

Getting clipped costs a life. You have 3. Caches glow green; the extraction beam unlocks at 10. Follow the gold arrow on your HUD or the markers on the minimap.
